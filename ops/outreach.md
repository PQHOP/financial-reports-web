## Google Publisher Center — draft submission

Verified 2026-10-25: `https://financialreportinsights.com/feed.xml` returns
200 with `application/rss+xml` (RSS 2.0). `/rss.xml` and `/feed` both 404.

- Publication name: Financial Report Insights
- Description (2-3 sentences): Plain-English analyses of US public
  companies' quarterly and annual SEC filings, written from the 10-Q, 10-K
  or earnings release itself. Every report carries an earnings-quality
  verdict (clean, flattered or understated) explaining what the headline
  numbers hide. During earnings season the site adds weekly earnings
  previews, head-to-head comparisons and sector scorecards built from the
  published reports' figures.
- Primary URL: https://financialreportinsights.com
- Sections:
  - Insights (editorial: previews, comparisons, market briefs) —
    https://financialreportinsights.com/insights
  - Reports (company report analyses) —
    https://financialreportinsights.com/reports
- RSS feed: https://financialreportinsights.com/feed.xml
- Language: English
- Location: Japan (publisher/operator); coverage: United States
- Contact email: the address shown on /contact (CONTACT_EMAIL)

Steps in Publisher Center (as best known; the UI changes — follow the
on-screen prompts if they differ):
1. Go to https://publishercenter.google.com and sign in with the Google
   account that already owns the Search Console property for
   financialreportinsights.com (ownership is verified through Search Console).
2. Click "Add publication", enter the name "Financial Report Insights" and
   the primary URL; choose Japan as location and English as language.
3. Under the publication's settings fill in: description, category
   (Business / Finance), contact email, and upload a square logo and a
   rectangular logo (PNG, light and dark variants if asked).
4. Under Google News → "Content settings" add sections: for each, choose
   "Feed" and paste https://financialreportinsights.com/feed.xml, or "Web
   location" and paste the /insights and /reports URLs; give each a label.
5. Review the preview, accept the content policies, and click "Publish" /
   "Submit for review". Note: since 2024 Google has said it no longer
   manually adds publications to Google News through Publisher Center —
   eligibility is decided algorithmically — so the realistic outcome of
   this step is a complete publication profile, not a guaranteed listing.
6. Afterwards, keep the feed and sitemap healthy, keep `NewsArticle`
   JSON-LD on dated articles (market briefs already emit it), and check the
   publication's status in Publisher Center every few weeks.
